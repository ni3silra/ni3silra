## DB Internet Transformation

Although I am part of db-Internet, I have had the opportunity to contribute to many projects, from legacy systems to modern cloud platforms.

DB Internet is a critical platform serving 16 countries and connected to more than 1,000 interfaces.

As a Technical Transformation Lead, my focus has been to bring the HP NonStop platform up to modern Deutsche Bank standards.

One of the first areas I worked on was the deployment process.

The platform was still using the legacy Revision Management System with many manual steps. Developers had to package components and move them through environments manually.

I modernized this process using Bitbucket and Jenkins, helping us move towards CI/CD.

This reduced manual effort, improved release stability, and made deployments more reliable.

I also led key migrations involving Core Limits, Bookings, and Instant Payment flows.

## ESA Migration

Another important initiative was the ESA Migration.

ESA supports the buying and selling of physical currency and precious metals for Deutsche Bank, ReiseBank, and Commerzbank.

The platform faced challenges due to legacy technology and operational delays.

I led the technical migration, working closely with business and external banking teams to understand their needs and translate them into technical solutions.

Along with the integration of Cinovo, which moved manual operational activities towards automation, this migration helped improve efficiency and retain important external banking business.

For me, this project strongly reflects client centricity and taking the client's perspective when delivering solutions.

## Kannon Proxy

Another project I am proud of is Kannon Proxy.

Kannon Proxy acts as a sidecar service for db-Internet and provides updated interest rate information from the Kannon application.

The challenge was that connecting Kannon directly through HP NonStop Kerberos would have resulted in significant costs.

As part of the team that built this solution, we chose a sidecar approach that delivered the same business outcome while reducing operational costs.

Today, I continue to lead this product through migrations, audits, compliance activities, and modernization efforts.

## Fabric Platform Migration

A major transformation initiative was the migration from Fabric OCP3 to OCP4.

This was required as teams moved towards modern Red Hat platforms.

I led the migration of:
- Kannon Proxy
- DBI Booking API
- Collector Booking API
- Supporting sidecar applications

At the same time, we also supported database consolidation by moving from multiple databases to a single platform.

This helped reduce infrastructure costs, simplify architecture, and lower operational overhead.

---

## Accounting Flow Simplification

One of the most valuable simplification initiatives involved the accounting ecosystem.

The DBI Booking API and Collector Booking API were mainly used to transform data between strategic and legacy systems.

These applications required infrastructure, support teams, audits, compliance activities, and ongoing maintenance.

To simplify this landscape, I led the migration that resulted in the DBI Booking API being decommissioned and merged into Booking SEP.

The same work is now underway for the Collector Booking API.

This has helped reduce infrastructure costs, lower maintenance effort, and move us closer to decommissioning legacy systems.

---

## New Relic for HP NonStop

One of my most recent achievements has been introducing New Relic observability for HP NonStop.

The challenge was that New Relic did not have a native HP NonStop agent.

Instead of accepting this limitation, I explored alternative options and designed a custom APM solution specifically for HP NonStop.

This solution provides monitoring for db-Internet workloads and brings important platform data into New Relic.

This has been a significant step towards modernization and gives us better visibility into our systems while supporting future simplification and decommissioning initiatives.
